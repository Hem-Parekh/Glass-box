# Surface brief: Aegis Ascend website and workspace

Scope: site/ (public pages = Persuade; signed-in workspace = Operate). Same page structure and features as before; rebrand and redesign only, plus CMA tool and Equity vs Debt Optimizer in the founder and advisor workspaces, and a founders section.

Audience and job: SME founders deciding debt vs equity; investors and lenders evaluating; the Aegis Ascend team operating the workspace. Primary action: book an engagement; secondary: open the workspace.

Proof on hand: the live, clickable model (every number explains itself), the 8-step method, three named co-founders with photos, real fee schedule. No clients, testimonials or results exist; never invent them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The emblem is the architecture. The hero is built inside the mark's own geometry: two gold uprights frame the content column, the outer diagonals run to the viewport edges, the chevron carries the live decision, and the diamond is the single point of decision used everywhere after. Refuses the fintech template: light hero, dashboard screenshot, row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian ground #0D0E10 with a faint brushed grain; gold ramp #E8C77B / #C9A04F / #8E6A2A; warm white text #F1ECE2, secondary #A8A193; gold hairlines. Cinzel (the logo's lettering) for display and wordmark in spaced capitals, Hanken Grotesk for reading and data. The gold diamond marks the recommended value, the active place and the chosen option. Workspace: obsidian rail, light stone work area #F3F2EF, white panels, deep-gold text #7E5F22 on light.

STORY: Visitor meets the firm and its promise (Smarter Decisions. Stronger Futures.), sees the method work on a live example, learns what the name and mark mean, meets Hem, Yash and Vivan, sees services and fees, then books or signs in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark field. Emblem geometry at viewport scale: uprights at the content column's edges, diagonals out to the corners, diamond above the H1. Centered between the uprights: tagline as H1 in Cinzel, one offer line, primary "Book an engagement" and secondary "Try the live model". Across the chevron's base: the live decision strip (Kalyan Polymers, Rs 12 cr: 60% debt / 40% equity) with clickable numbers.

FORM: Brief-pinned world (logo palette, emblem geometry, existing site template); no concept roll. seed: none (unavailable)
Signature motion: the Ascent. On load the emblem draws bottom-up (stroke reveal, 900ms, ease-out-expo), the diamond lands last, the gold rules extend from center, then the decision strip fills to 60/40. Reduced motion: lines appear drawn, a short fade only. Workspace motion is feedback only (150-250ms).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
